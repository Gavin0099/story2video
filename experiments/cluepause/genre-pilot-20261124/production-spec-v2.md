## Image and framing contract

Render at 1080×1920, 9:16, 30 FPS, H.264/AAC. Every shot is full-bleed realistic photography with the actual action filling the portrait canvas: hands cutting, a moving needle, water reaching soles, a voice reaction, or a loaded door. Use readable exposure even in the horror scene. No retro illustration, square panel in a 500×500 box, letterbox/pillarbox, card frame, border, blurred duplicate background, `fit=True`, contain mode, or textbook diagram. A naturally blurred photographic background is fine; an artificial background used to pad a fitted panel is not.

Use portrait source images or a checked cover crop/region that fills the canvas. Inspect all four edges throughout any movement. Keep the necessary clue inside the final crop with enough depth of field and screen area to be recognized once on a phone. If cover cropping loses the hand, scale pan, thread, wet/dry contrast, latch or evidence text on a prop, recompose the source shot; do not shrink the image into a box. A 2×3 sheet is an asset container, not an output layout. No sheet gutters may survive extraction. Remove irrelevant signs and added explanatory lettering. Readable labels, phone messages and intercom text are allowed when they are themselves in-world evidence, identified as such in the shot list. The U01 clock currently uses hands and tick marks; no added label is needed.

Follow the scripts’ framing changes: extreme close-ups, object/hand inserts, eye-line and over-shoulder views, face reactions, low side angles and a short causal proof action. No sequence of near-identical frontal symmetrical wides. Target cuts range from a 0.5 s impact insert to 2–5 s evidence shots; P01’s rapid opening is an intentional exception to ordinary reading time. Do not equalize all shots or add a cut without a new observation. Subtle motion must not drift across a clue or substitute for the scripted event. A zoom on a still of scissors is not a visible snip; a high/low needle jump is not evidence of a continuous response. Plan photographic action footage or correctly ordered action states with clear continuity; reject a reconstruction whose action cannot be read. Source generation and its feasibility have not been tested in this task.

These are scripts, not measured recordings. Use ordinary, natural spoken delivery; narration does not have to occupy every interval. H01’s dialogue, repeated cough and playback are part of its audio timing. Avoid overlapping intelligible voices. The rule is **no blank stretch without a narrative purpose**. Ambient sound, a character reaction, evidence inspection or a suspense pause can carry that purpose, especially in urban_horror. Background sound alone does not establish a purpose: specify what the viewer is noticing or anticipating. P01's current hold invites comparison of the gold and needle, U01's the wet/dry contrast, and H01's the imminent reply beside the listening device. Their approximately two-second pre-reveal holds are initial design values and may be shortened, lengthened or omitted when the story's rhythm supports it. Do not add an empty intro, purposeless still hold, end card or silent tail.

On a later authorized production, record/synthesize and listen to the spoken audio, then derive SRT cues from the actual spoken boundaries and edit shots around both that audio and purposeful action/holds, using make.py’s cue-driven approach where applicable. Keep first concrete action within 0–1 s and the question at about 1 s. Aim for 30–40 s overall; the current 36/36/39 s tables are initial plans. Assess a short spoken track against the whole narrative: retain meaningful reactions, ambient evidence and suspense rather than automatically adding narration. If the remaining time has no narrative purpose, shorten the edit or revise meaningful story copy and repeat the applicable blind gate. If long, tighten and re-gate rather than rush delivery, time-stretch beyond natural speech or truncate clues. A script change after reader testing requires a new frozen version and fresh readers. After timing, regenerate the script/packet timestamps together, including any changed pause or reveal boundary; no independently drifting reader text.

At proof review, map speech, action, ambient evidence, reactions and suspense across the whole timeline. Inspect multi-second speech gaps for narrative purpose; do not reject a purposeful beat merely because speech is absent or automatically accept it because music/room tone is present. Reject stretches with no narrative purpose. Measure the final voice length and cue gaps to inform that inspection, rather than treating speech coverage as acceptance. No TTS or timing measurement was performed for this draft.
