## 1. What is the video asking?

Why do shoes put away dry in a locked cabinet become wet only on their soles in the morning, despite nobody else having the key?

## 2. Most likely answer

**A leak in the plant-watering hose inside the cabinet, activated by the morning watering timer — 88%.** The hose loops through the rear vent, giving water a route inside without opening the lock. The timer clicks at six, which fits the morning pattern. Water collecting in the shallow tray explains the wet soles and dry laces, and overflowing or draining from the cabinet explains the puddle directly beneath its seam while the surrounding hallway stays dry. I suspect a small hole or loose connection in the hose, although neither is actually shown.

## 3. Reasonable alternatives

- **Water follows the outside of the hose from the watering setup into the cabinet — 9%.** A spill or overflow above could run along the hose and drip from its low loop into the tray. The route and timing fit, but no water traveling along the hose is visible.
- **Condensation on the hose drips into the tray — 3%.** A cold hose could collect moisture inside the cabinet, leaving the shoe uppers dry. It is a weaker explanation for the amount of water and the precise morning timing; the video gives no temperature evidence.
