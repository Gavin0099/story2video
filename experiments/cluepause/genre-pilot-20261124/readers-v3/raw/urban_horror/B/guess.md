# 1. What is the main conflict or threat?
Something outside Nia’s locked door is copying her mother’s voice and may get inside if the last two words of Nia’s first reply form an invitation.

# 2. The rules of this world, and anything that seems inconsistent
- After midnight, the intercom records your first reply but keeps only its last two words.
- If the saved words are an invitation, the door unlocks.
- You cannot replace that recording until dawn, so a careless reply could leave you unable to undo it.
- The thing can speak through the intercom without anyone pressing a button. It can also copy a cough that happens inside the room, which makes it seem able to hear them.
- The locked door is currently holding despite pressure on the handle.

I’m unclear whether the intercom listens to all speech in the room or only speech addressed to it. Mom’s cough gets copied, but apparently does not count as the first reply; perhaps only words count, or only Nia’s response does. I also don’t know how it decides what qualifies as an invitation. None of this is a definite contradiction yet, but those boundaries matter a lot.

# 3. What I expect or fear will happen next, and why
I fear Nia will blurt out something that sounds safe as a whole but becomes an invitation when shortened. “Don’t come in” is the obvious danger: the saved words would be “come in.” The emphasis on her uncovered mouth and the waiting light makes me expect her next words to be the decisive moment.

Alternatively, she might deliberately use a reply ending in an unmistakable refusal, using up the recording before panic makes her say something worse. I would still worry about Mom waking and answering the familiar voice if the device can accept anyone’s reply. The copied cough also makes me fear that the thing is learning how to make its impersonation more convincing.

# 4. When unease started, and what caused it
Almost immediately: the handle moves with Nia’s hand nowhere near it. Hearing Mom’s voice from the speaker while seeing Mom asleep establishes that the danger is stranger than an ordinary intruder. The delayed copy of her cough is the strongest escalation for me, because it suggests the thing is listening inside their home right now.
