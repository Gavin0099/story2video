**Premise:** Retained: shoes become wet each morning inside a locked cabinet. The plant’s timed watering line leaks into the tray under their soles; nobody has worn them outside.

**Reveal / author-only causal summary:** A cracked connector in the plant-watering hose leaks during the six-o’clock pump cycle. Water follows the cabinet’s rear wall into the shoe tray, wetting soles but not uppers or laces. The lock never needed to open.

## Author / host notes — never distribute to blind readers

All events are chronological. “She’d put them away dry” establishes the prior condition without a calendar placard. “At six next day” identifies the later observation in audio. The shoe cabinet also serves as a plant stand. A real slack loop of its watering hose passes through a generous rear ventilation slot and back up to the pot; seed this ordinary routing at 19 s. The connector sits inside the loop near the rear wall, above the tray but behind the shoes. Water must trickle down the rear wall into the tray, not spray across laces. The route and connector must fit the same cabinet at both angles; do not invent a second pipe in the reveal.

Fair evidence before 25 s: closed lock with actual dripping at 0.3 s; sole-only wetness at 7 s; dry surrounding floor at 11 s (apart from the cabinet drip puddle); scheduled pump and clock at 15 s; water-carrying hose entering the cabinet at 19 s. The conjunction makes the plant system a reasoned hypothesis. No leak, crack, traced water path, “the plant watered the shoes” line or diagram appears before 27 s. A viewer guessing irrigation from these clues is allowed; hiding the entire system to prevent guesses would be unfair.

Reasonable alternatives include condensation or another plumbing leak. The fixed morning pump cycle and dry floor narrow them; the repeated click plus visible seep, route and wet soles in the reveal establish this particular cause. Host core: the timed watering system leaks into the tray inside the locked cabinet. “Someone wore them” contradicts the revealed causal sequence. No ghost tail, dream or unseen roommate negates it.
