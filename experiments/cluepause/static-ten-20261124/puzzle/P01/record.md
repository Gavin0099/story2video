### Canonical mechanism, defined before generation

The complete specification is [mechanism.md](revisions/v2/mechanism.md), with canonical photographic prop [mechanism-reference.png](revisions/v2/assets/mechanism-reference.png). **A** is the knot in the eyelet on the underside of the **moving pan**, **G** is the smooth sliding hole in the **horizontal countertop**, and **B** is the toe loop. The sole route is **A → vertically through G → vertically down to B**. A and B are the only load-bearing endpoints. G is a guide, never an anchor; there are no other anchors, pulleys, horizontal segments or counter-edge attachments. The stationary scale base does not intersect the thread. The cylinder stands independently on a low shelf to the right, clear of the thread corridor; its ring is empty.

A pressed-down toe tensions the thread and pulls down on the moving pan, adding force to the gold's weight; the pan is lower and needle upper-right. Severing below G leaves the upper end freely hanging and the toe end slack. The pan rises and the needle returns lower-left. The image shows a two-state comparison, not continuous motion or a calibrated numerical reading. The original gold invariant is normalized descriptively to the actual repeated disc/triangle/rectangle; the disc is round rather than a distinct oval.
