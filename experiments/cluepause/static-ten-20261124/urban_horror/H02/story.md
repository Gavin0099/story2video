# FICTION — H02: The Smallest Mirror

Genre: urban_horror
Rights: pending_verification
Original invented fiction; no real people, events, brands, or source-work recreation. No gore.
Target: 36.0 seconds; English narration, English and Traditional Chinese sidecars.
Opening question only at 1.0–3.5 seconds: Why is she covering the mirror? / 她為何遮住鏡子？

## World rule
When the lift doors close, the passenger behind the glass can cross through an uncovered reflection. The rule applies to reflections, not just the large wall mirror.

## Alternative and fair clues
A loose white coat seems to be moving over the wall mirror; the plausible threat appears to remain behind that large mirror.
The same grey fingers press against glass; the phone is present from the first frame but initially lit. Its screen goes black only after the coat covers the large mirror.

## In-world evidence text
No photographed lettering. The phone screen is an ordinary blank light screen until shot-05, then black reflective glass.

## Script
0–5.5s: Mira was alone. The other passenger was behind the mirror.
Mira 獨自在電梯裡。另一名乘客卻在鏡子後面。

5.5–11s: When the doors closed, it could cross through an uncovered reflection.
門一關，它就能穿過沒有遮住的反射面。

11–17s: She held the doors open and pulled off her coat.
她按住開門鍵，脫下外套。

17–23s: She covered the glass. Something kept pressing underneath.
她遮住鏡面。底下仍有東西頂著布。

23–28.5s: Her phone went dark. She finally let the doors close.
手機螢幕暗了。她終於鬆手讓門關上。

28.5–36s: The fingers came through the smallest mirror.
手指穿過了最小的那面鏡子。
