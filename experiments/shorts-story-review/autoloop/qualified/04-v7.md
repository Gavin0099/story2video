# 04 v7｜停在姓氏前

## 繁中題卡

### 第一段

我剛把名字念完，哥哥就刪了我的試音。那份有報酬的配音工作只剩我們兩個在競爭。他的試音已經交了。我只剩兩分鐘。

### 第二段

哥哥原本答應替我念一句對手戲。重錄時，我在姓氏前停了一下，還是把名字念完了。他只回放那一秒。我的姓疊著兩道聲音，他的早了一拍。他的手指又放回停止鍵。

離截止都這麼近了，哥哥為什麼還要我重來？

## 揭曉

我又在姓氏前卡住。他張開了嘴。手指離開停止鍵，抵住自己的嘴唇。錄音燈還亮著。我把名字念完，他才念答應陪我的那一句。

錄完，他把滑鼠推到我面前。我把有停頓的那一遍送出去。離截止還有三秒。

## 英文旁白

I’d barely finished saying my name when my brother deleted my audition. The two of us were up for the same paid voice job. His audition was already in. I had two minutes left.

He’d promised to read one line opposite me. On the next take I stalled before my last name, but got it out. He replayed that second. Two voices said my last name. His was a beat ahead. His finger went back to Stop. Why make me start over with so little time left?

I stalled in the same place. He opened his mouth. His finger left Stop and pressed against his own lips. The red light stayed on. I finished my name. He read the line he’d promised. Then he slid the mouse toward me. I sent the take with the pause still in it. Three seconds to spare.
